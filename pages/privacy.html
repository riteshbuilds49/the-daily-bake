Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Daily Bake ("we", "us", "our") handles your information when you visit thedailybake.vercel.app (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address: if you sign up for regular updates using the form in our footer.
  • Messages and order details: if you contact us or place an order through WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and whatever you choose to write in the chat.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic information such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited.

We do not ask for payment card details or passwords on this Site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you updates about new products, offers and bakery news (only if you signed up).
  • To reply to your messages and process your orders.
  • To keep the Site secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

The Site uses the following services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel: hosts the Site and may process technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Google Maps: an embedded map shows our location. Google may collect data and set cookies when the map loads.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you click our WhatsApp link, your conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Cookies

We do not set our own cookies to track you. However, third-party services on the Site, such as the embedded Google Map, may use cookies or similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies from your browser settings.

5. Data Retention

We keep your email address until you ask us to remove it or you unsubscribe. Messages sent via WhatsApp are kept only as long as needed to handle your request or order.

6. Your Choices

  • You can unsubscribe from updates at any time by emailing us.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy or your data, email us at: contact@thedailybake.com.
